1 Overview & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Bharat Adhikar mobile application ("App", "we", "us", "our") operated by an independent developer based in India. The App provides citizens with tools to file government complaints, track election promises, crowd-source infrastructure audits (Pramaan), and draft formal letters to authorities.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy. If you disagree, please uninstall the App.

Applicable law: This policy is governed by the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, India. Where applicable, we also align with international standards including GDPR principles for users accessing from the EU.

3 How We Use Your Data

We use collected data only for the following purposes:

  • Complaint generation: Your spoken or typed complaint is processed by an AI model to draft a formal letter. This processing happens via our AI proxy (Supabase Edge Function) and the generated text is returned to your device. We do not store the raw voice audio or the raw complaint text on our servers.
  • Pramaan audit map: Your submitted audit photo, GPS coordinates, and description are stored in Firebase to display on the public map. Your Firebase anonymous UID is attached as a Citizen ID — never your name or email.
  • Election promise tracking: Your vote (verified/discrepancy) is written as an atomic increment to Firestore. No identifier beyond the anonymous UID is attached.
  • Local feed filtering: Your GPS coordinates are used client-side and for the Firestore query. Raw coordinates are not stored separately.
  • App improvement: Aggregated, non-personal crash and performance data via Firebase.
  • Ad delivery: Google AdMob uses device signals to serve contextual ads. We do not pass any complaint content to AdMob.
We never use your data for: profiling, political targeting, selling to third parties, training our own AI models on your content, or any purpose not listed above.

4 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • With infrastructure providers (Google Firebase, Supabase) solely to operate the App — governed by their respective data processing agreements.
  • With Google AdMob for ad serving — AdMob may collect its own data under Google's privacy policy.
  • With AI model providers (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Groq, Mistral, Cohere) to generate complaint letters. Only the text you provide for complaint generation is sent. These providers process the request and return the result; they are contractually prohibited from storing your queries for training without consent.
  • Public audit data: When you submit a Pramaan audit, the photo, description, and location are visible to all app users on the map. This is an intentional, consent-based feature — you confirm before submitting.
  • Legal requirement: We may disclose information if required by Indian law, court order, or to prevent fraud or imminent harm.

6 Location Data

Location access is requested at runtime and is entirely optional. If denied, the App defaults to a national India view and all features remain available except local-radius filtering.

  • GPS coordinates are used on-device to center the map and filter the Local feed.
  • When you submit a Pramaan audit, your coordinates at time of submission are stored in Firestore and displayed publicly on the map. You are informed of this before submission.
  • We do not continuously track your location in the background.
  • Raw GPS coordinates are never sent to advertising networks.

7 Voice & Speech Data

The complaint flow uses your device microphone to capture speech via Android's native Speech Recognition API. This is handled entirely by your device's OS and Google's Speech-to-Text service (if using Android's default recognizer).

  • We do not record, store, or transmit your raw voice audio.
  • The transcribed text is displayed to you before being processed by the AI.
  • You can edit or discard the transcript at any time before proceeding.
  • The transcribed text is processed by the AI letter-generation pipeline and then discarded — only the generated letter is returned to your device.
Microphone permission is requested only when you tap the microphone button. The App does not access your microphone at any other time.

8 Anonymity & Account Information

Bharat Adhikar uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication. When you first open the App, a random anonymous UID is generated. This UID:

  • Is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any real-world identity.
  • Is stored only on your device and in Firebase Auth.
  • Is used as a "Citizen ID" when you submit audits — visible to other users in that format.
  • Can be reset by clearing the App's data or reinstalling.

We do not offer account registration. There is no login, no email address required, and no password. This is by design.

10 Security

We implement the following security measures in line with industry standards:

  • TLS/HTTPS for all network communication between the App and Firebase/Supabase.
  • Supabase Edge Functions as a secure proxy for all AI API calls — your device never holds AI provider API keys.
  • Firebase Security Rules to restrict read/write access to Firestore and Firebase Storage by authenticated (anonymous) users only.
  • No hardcoded secrets in the App binary — all sensitive keys are server-side.
  • Minimal data collection as the primary security measure — data we don't collect cannot be breached.

While we take reasonable precautions, no digital system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your information, we will notify affected users through the App and/or the Play Store listing within a reasonable timeframe as required by Indian law.
